# Fintech Signup Form Usability Research - Discussion Guide

## Research Objectives
- Understand current signup pain points and abandonment factors
- Identify key decision factors for fintech service adoption
- Test form usability and user flow effectiveness
- Validate target user needs and expectations
- Gather specific improvement recommendations

## Target Participants
- **Primary**: Novice users (25-40) with limited fintech experience
- **Secondary**: Experienced users for comparison and validation
- **Tertiary**: Power users to understand advanced needs

## Interview Structure (60 minutes total)

### 1. Introduction & Warm-up (5 minutes)
**Goals**: Build rapport, understand participant context

**Questions**:
- Tell me about yourself and how you currently manage your finances
- What financial apps or services do you use regularly?
- How do you typically research and choose new financial products?
- What's your comfort level with new technology and financial services?

### 2. Current Experience Deep Dive (15 minutes)
**Goals**: Understand real user experiences and pain points

**Questions**:
- Walk me through the last time you signed up for a new financial service
- What was the most frustrating part of that signup process?
- What information were you comfortable sharing vs. hesitant about?
- How did you verify your identity during signup?
- What made you trust (or not trust) the service during signup?

**Follow-up probes**:
- Can you give me a specific example of when you abandoned a signup?
- What would have made you complete it?
- How did you feel about the security measures?

### 3. Pain Points & Challenges (10 minutes)
**Goals**: Identify specific barriers and concerns

**Questions**:
- What typically makes you abandon a signup process?
- What security concerns do you have when signing up for financial services?
- How do you prefer to receive verification codes or confirmations?
- What would make you trust a new fintech service more?
- How important is speed vs. security in the signup process?

**Follow-up probes**:
- Tell me about a time when security concerns stopped you from signing up
- What information do you consider "too personal" to share upfront?
- How do you verify if a financial service is legitimate?

### 4. Ideal Experience Vision (10 minutes)
**Goals**: Understand user preferences and ideal solutions

**Questions**:
- If you could design the perfect signup experience, what would it look like?
- What information would you want to see upfront before starting signup?
- How would you prefer to complete identity verification?
- What would make you feel confident about completing the signup?
- How would you want to be guided through the process?

**Follow-up probes**:
- What would make you feel most secure during signup?
- How would you want to be notified about progress?
- What would make the process feel trustworthy?

### 5. Specific Feature Testing (10 minutes)
**Goals**: Test specific design concepts and features

**Questions**:
- How would you feel about a progress indicator during signup?
- What's your preference for password requirements?
- How would you want to handle two-factor authentication?
- What would make you comfortable sharing your bank account information?
- How important is it to see security badges or certifications?

### 6. Wrap-up & Next Steps (5 minutes)
**Goals**: Gather final thoughts and maintain relationship

**Questions**:
- Is there anything else about signup experiences you'd like to share?
- What questions do you have about our research?
- Would you be interested in participating in future research?
- How can we follow up with you about our findings?

## Key Areas to Probe

### Trust & Security
- Security indicators and certifications
- Data handling and privacy policies
- Identity verification methods
- Communication about security measures

### Usability & Flow
- Form design and field requirements
- Progress indication and guidance
- Error handling and recovery
- Mobile vs. desktop experience

### Information Architecture
- What information to request when
- How to present complex terms
- Onboarding and education
- Help and support availability

### Emotional Factors
- Anxiety and stress points
- Confidence building measures
- Social proof and testimonials
- Risk communication

## Success Metrics
- **Completion Rate**: Target 25% improvement
- **Time to Complete**: Reduce by 30%
- **User Satisfaction**: 4.5+ rating
- **Trust Score**: 80%+ confidence level
- **Abandonment Points**: Identify and address top 3

## Notes for Moderator
- Use the "think aloud" method when appropriate
- Probe for specific examples and stories
- Pay attention to emotional responses
- Note body language and hesitation points
- Ask "why" to understand underlying motivations
- Use laddering technique for deeper insights
